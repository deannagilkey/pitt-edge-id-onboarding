Course Overview

A Performance-Support Course for New Pitt EDGE Designers

This Canvas course onboards new Pitt EDGE instructional designers during their first 30–90 days, then stays in place afterward as an ongoing performance-support resource — the same page a designer returns to six months in when they forget which SharePoint site holds the project template.

All 12 planned modules are now built out in full below — from onboarding fundamentals through assessment, quality assurance, course maintenance, the closing knowledge check and completion certificate, and Module 11, a role-specific learning experience on Pitt Professional added after the original map was set.

Full Scope

The Full Course Map

Modules 0 through 10, shown here as they exist in Canvas today. This is the real module list, not a planning document — every title below is a page a new designer will actually open.

Module 0

Onboarding & Operations

Everything a new designer needs before touching a course build: who's who, how the team communicates, what's mandatory, and what technology they'll have access to. The module front-loads logistics so later modules can stay focused on actual design and development work.

Module 1

Pitt EDGE Programs & Partnerships

Grounds a new designer in the actual academic landscape they'll support — real schools, real programs, real staffing models — before any process training begins. A designer who knows what OneID, Pillar, and Legacy programs actually are can prioritize their own work correctly from day one.

Module 2

Course Development Lifecycle

The four-phase methodology every course built at Pitt EDGE follows: Design → Develop → Deploy/Term Prep → Launch. Each phase is anchored to a named checkpoint and a single deliverable that defines what 'done' actually means, so handoffs between faculty, designers, and QA don't depend on anyone's memory.

Module 3

Starting & Managing a Project

Instructional designers at Pitt EDGE serve as project managers for their own course developments — coordinating faculty, deliverables, timelines, reviews, and handoffs from kickoff through launch. This module covers what to review when a new assignment lands, the systems used to track it, and how to close it out cleanly when it's done.

Module 4

Developing Courses in Canvas

The operational core of the course: how a designer actually builds in Canvas at Pitt EDGE. Covers the three platforms in play, the three Canvas environments, shell naming and promotion, the master template, and the two tools (DesignPLUS and UDOIT) that shape and check the final build.

Module 5

Pitt EDGE Technology Stack & Interactive Learning Objects

Reframes Pitt EDGE's tools as one integrated learning ecosystem rather than a list of separate platforms, then covers how a designer selects the right one for a given learning need. The module's second half shifts from tool selection to scoping: classifying Interactive Learning Objects (ILOs) by complexity and documenting that complexity in the project charter before development starts.

Module 6

Multimedia

Gated behind Module 5 as a prerequisite, this module covers the media production workflows a designer coordinates alongside the Canvas build: video production, the Media Asset Tracker as system of record, captioning and accessibility compliance, copyright and licensing, and where AI policy currently stands at Pitt.

Module 7

Assessment, Academic Integrity & AI Guidance

Covers the policies, tools, and practices that shape assessment at Pitt EDGE: aligning assessments to learning objectives, applying academic-integrity and AI guidance, building assessments in Canvas and approved third-party systems (Respondus, ExamSoft), and recommending proportionate assessment-security measures.

Module 8

Quality Assurance, Course Review, and Launch

Gated behind Module 7 as a prerequisite, this module covers how a course actually gets checked and published: the quality frameworks Pitt EDGE belongs to, the OLC Course Review Scorecard, an accessibility review with UDOIT, and the term-prep workflow that carries a course from final sign-offs through faculty launch and hypercare support.

Module 9

Course Maintenance, Revisions, and Redesigns

Treats a launched course as something that keeps getting worked on: distinguishing maintenance, revision, and redesign projects, tracking them in TDX and Airtable, and reapplying quality and accessibility review after changes are made.

Module 10 · Conclusion

Course Conclusion

Closes out the onboarding course: a graded knowledge check followed by the course completion certificate, gated behind Module 9 as a prerequisite.

Module 11 · Role-Specific Learning Experience

Designing in Pitt Professional

A role-specific learning experience on Pitt Professional, the platform behind Pitt EDGE's non-credit, professional development, and continuing education offerings. Runs from platform fundamentals and governance through hands-on configuration in Canvas, Canvas Catalog, and Accredible.
